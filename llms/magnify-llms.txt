# Magnify

Magnify is an AI-powered Customer Growth Automation (CGA) platform that helps B2B SaaS revenue teams predict churn risk, uncover expansion opportunities, and automate engagement across the entire customer lifecycle — without adding headcount.

The platform connects signals from CRM systems, product usage, customer engagement, and revenue data to help Sales, Customer Success, Revenue Operations, and Growth teams operate with greater visibility and predictability.

Magnify focuses on helping organizations move from reactive post-sales management to predictive, automated revenue lifecycle orchestration. Unlike traditional Customer Success Platforms that produce health scores, Magnify forecasts actual dollar amounts at risk or available for expansion — and then acts on them automatically.
